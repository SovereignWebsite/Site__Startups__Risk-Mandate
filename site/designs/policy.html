3What it is

The product is the Agent Behaviour Policy, or ABP: a written record, for one agent in one deployment, of what the agent can do, what it was authorised to do, the gap between them, and the barrier on each.2

The ABP “describes and it does not judge, so it carries no score.” It is read-only and never sits in the agent's request path; enforcement, when wanted, runs on the customer's side.23

Its stated direction is insurability: from a behaviour policy, to a Licence to Operate, to an agent that can be insured. The company says plainly that the later steps are templates and designs, and “No insurer has agreed to anything on this page.”4

It sits beside security vendors rather than against them: “We don't detect. We don't remediate. We make the business own the risk.”5

4The products

  • Agent Behaviour Policy ↗

    The record for one agent in one deployment: reach, mandate, gap and barrier.28

  • ABP library ↗

    16 template policies published free, for tools such as Claude Code, GitHub Actions and the Microsoft 365 connector.11

  • SHarness ↗

    An open-source secure harness, Apache-2.0, that turns a policy into one the Linux kernel holds. The site says it is partly running.9

  • Licence to Operate ↗

    A template in every published vault; not yet issued.12

  • Insurability Index ↗

    A 0 to 100 score across five dimensions; a design, not yet a product.4

  • Customers site ↗

    “Put a behaviour policy on your agent, in five minutes”, with six examples for Claude or ChatGPT.13
