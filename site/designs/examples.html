Agent Behaviour Policies, as building blocks.

“The insurability layer for agentic AI.”

“An Agent Behaviour Policy describes one AI agent in one deployment: everything it can do (the grant), what you actually authorised (the mandate), the gap between the two (the delta), and what really stands in the way of each thing (the barrier).”

More of riskmandate.ai

What it is

The product is the Agent Behaviour Policy, or ABP: a written record, for one agent in one deployment, of what the agent can do, what it was authorised to do, the gap between them, and the barrier on each.

The ABP “describes and it does not judge, so it carries no score.” It is read-only and never sits in the agent's request path; enforcement, when wanted, runs on the customer's side.

Its stated direction is insurability: from a behaviour policy, to a Licence to Operate, to an agent that can be insured. The company says plainly that the later steps are templates and designs, and “No insurer has agreed to anything on this page.”

It sits beside security vendors rather than against them: “We don't detect. We don't remediate. We make the business own the risk.”

Who it is for

  • Anyone who runs AI agents, from one agent to hundreds.
  • People who gave an assistant access to a repository, a mailbox or a cloud account.
  • Investors with a portfolio, and companies that will be diligenced.
  • Underwriters, risk committees and boards, the audiences the brand is meant to speak to.

How it works

  1. The mandateWhat you authorised the agent to do, drawn out of you.
  2. The grantWhat the agent can actually reach, measured from the deployment.
  3. The deltaThe difference between the two, derived and never edited.
  4. The barrierWhat stands in the way, per capability: none, an expectation, a setting or a boundary. Only a boundary counts as a control.

Sources: 2

What it offers

  • Agent Behaviour Policy ↗

    The record for one agent in one deployment: reach, mandate, gap and barrier.

  • ABP library ↗

    16 template policies published free, for tools such as Claude Code, GitHub Actions and the Microsoft 365 connector.

  • SHarness ↗

    An open-source secure harness, Apache-2.0, that turns a policy into one the Linux kernel holds. The site says it is partly running.

  • Licence to Operate ↗

    A template in every published vault; not yet issued.

  • Insurability Index ↗

    A 0 to 100 score across five dimensions; a design, not yet a product.

  • Customers site ↗

    “Put a behaviour policy on your agent, in five minutes”, with six examples for Claude or ChatGPT.
