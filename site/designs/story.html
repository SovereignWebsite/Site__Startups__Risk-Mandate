The story of

RiskMandate

RiskMandate writes an Agent Behaviour Policy for each AI agent you run: everything it can actually reach, what you authorised it to do, the gap between the two, and what really stands in the way.

RiskMandate starts from a gap: an AI agent is given access to a repository, a mailbox or a cloud account, and what it can reach is far more than what anyone meant it to do.6 2

Its site began on 29 June 2026 and became its own repository with version 1.0.0 on 11 September. A week later the company exhibited at Startup Summit in Lisbon, on 17 and 18 September.7 6

In October it published its investor deck under CC BY 4.0 and, on 29 October, introduced SHarness, an open-source harness that has the Linux kernel enforce a policy.8 9

At Web Summit in Lisbon in November 2026 it ran its own team of agents on the stand, most with their own behaviour policy. On 10 November the circuit showed 109 agents, 94 of them with a written policy.10

The company is candid about where it is. Early trading runs through The Cyber Boardroom, a UK company, and "a company for RiskMandate alone, between the two founders, is being set up." No customer is named.8 10

How it works

  1. The mandateWhat you authorised the agent to do, drawn out of you.2
  2. The grantWhat the agent can actually reach, measured from the deployment.2
  3. The deltaThe difference between the two, derived and never edited.2
  4. The barrierWhat stands in the way, per capability: none, an expectation, a setting or a boundary. Only a boundary counts as a control.2

What it offers

  • Agent Behaviour Policy

    The record for one agent in one deployment: reach, mandate, gap and barrier.2 8

  • ABP library

    16 template policies published free, for tools such as Claude Code, GitHub Actions and the Microsoft 365 connector.11

  • SHarness

    An open-source secure harness, Apache-2.0, that turns a policy into one the Linux kernel holds. The site says it is partly running.9

  • Licence to Operate

    A template in every published vault; not yet issued.12

  • Insurability Index

    A 0 to 100 score across five dimensions; a design, not yet a product.4

  • Customers site

    "Put a behaviour policy on your agent, in five minutes", with six examples for Claude or ChatGPT.13
